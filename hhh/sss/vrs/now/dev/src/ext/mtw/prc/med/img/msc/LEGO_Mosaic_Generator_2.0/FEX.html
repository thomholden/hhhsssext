LEGO Mosaic Generator 2.0

by Shaun VanWeelden

 

11 Feb 2013

Turn any image into a LEGO mosaic with building instructions with this improved mosaic generator

| Watch this File

File Information
Description

This File Exchange entry comes with 4 different functions:

- generate_colormap; used for picking colors you want in your mosaic and naming them simultaneously for your parts list

- pre_processing; guides you through the process of getting your source image into a mosaic-able format. You specify things such as how tall and wide it will be, as well as the color map used.

- post_processing; the real mosaic-building begins. You have the option of watching your mosaic come to life as it automatically fits the correct Lego pieces in your image. In the end, you are left with a saved "instruction manual" showing you where all the pieces go and it even provides you a parts list and estimated cost of the mosaic.

- mosaic_color_map, this function serves as a color map utility for the other functions.

Each function is extremely well documented within, and you can always feel free to contact me with any other questions or suggestions.

Good luck and happy building!
