Instructions:

1. Give the symbol of the stock .
2. Give todays date in the specific format (months-days-year).
3. Press the 'GET DATA' button. 

    Sometimes the Yahoo server does not respond and you'll get an error from the StockQuoteQuery function.
    (Michael Boldin - http://www.mathworks.nl/matlabcentral/fileexchange/loadFile.do?objectId=4069&objectType=file).
    Try again!

4. Choose the number of days you want to examine.
5. Pick the fast and slow averages used by the functions (remember fast has to be smaller than slow).
6. Press the 'RESULTS' button.
7. You can update the results using the days/fast/slow options and re-press 'RESULTS'.
